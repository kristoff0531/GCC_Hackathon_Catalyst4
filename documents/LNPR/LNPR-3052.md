# LNPR-3052 — Rate Calculation Failure

## Symptom

Loan pricing requests fail during interest-rate calculation.

## Root Cause

The reference-rate service is unavailable or returning incomplete rate data.

## Fix

1. Check the reference-rate service health dashboard.
2. Verify that the required rate data is available.
3. Review LNPR application logs for rate lookup failures.
4. Escalate to Lending Platform Engineering if the service remains unavailable.
5. Reprocess the request according to the approved runbook.

## Do NOT

Do NOT manually enter or override an interest rate without an approved business procedure.
