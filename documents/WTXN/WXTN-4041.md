# WTXN-4041 — Beneficiary Validation Timeout

## Symptom

Wire submission hangs during beneficiary verification and fails after 30 seconds.

## Root Cause

The downstream SWIFT/BIC registry lookup service (SWFVAL) is slow or unresponsive.

## Fix

1. Check the SWFVAL health dashboard.
2. Verify whether the registry lookup service is responding.
3. Escalate to Treasury Operations Engineering if the service remains unavailable.
4. Enable cached-registry mode according to the approved runbook.

## Do NOT

Do NOT manually override beneficiary validation and force-submit the wire.

This is a compliance violation.

## Escalation

Treasury Operations Engineering
