# CCDS-2107 — Credit Decision Timeout

## Symptom

Customer credit applications remain in processing state and eventually time out.

## Root Cause

The downstream credit bureau service is responding slowly or is temporarily unavailable.

## Fix

1. Check the credit bureau service health dashboard.
2. Review recent CCDS application logs for timeout errors.
3. Verify whether the downstream service is responding.
4. Escalate to Credit Decisioning Engineering if the issue persists.
5. Retry the application only according to the approved runbook.

## Do NOT

Do NOT manually approve a credit application when the automated decisioning service is unavailable.

Credit Decisioning Engineering
